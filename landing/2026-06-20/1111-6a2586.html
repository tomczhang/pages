FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You ask your AI agent in plain language; the mdsite skill does the rest and gives you a link.

Where does my content live?

On your own GitHub Pages (free path) — we host nothing. Or on mdsite Cloud if you choose the hosted Pro option.

What if I don't have GitHub?

That's what hosted Pro is for — one click, no GitHub, instant link. Join the waitlist above.

Which agents are supported?

Any agent that supports skills — Codex, WorkBuddy, Claude, Raft, and more being added.
